    About InTheBox.es
    An independent creative-technology project. Its current experiment is a deterministic generative microworld.
    Earlier work
    InTheBox.es began in 2015. Earlier work involved hand-built computers and reclaimed and unconventional materials, with concerns around art, reuse and technology. The current experiment is a deterministic generative microworld on the web.
